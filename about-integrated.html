Built Exclusively For Commercial Leasing.

Leasync unifies listings, leads, tenants, tours, deal flow, commissions, and email into one elegant operating system designed for retail and restaurant brokerages.

Whether you’re an independent broker, a boutique firm, or a large corporate brokerage, Leasync transforms the way you manage your business.

Why we built this

Most leasing brokers run their whole business out of spreadsheets, group chats, and memory. That works until it doesn’t — a tour goes unlogged, a follow-up slips, a commission check nobody chased. So we built Leasync in NYC, side by side with working brokers, to put listings, tenants, tours, deals, and commissions in one place.

— Aaron & Mason, co-founders
